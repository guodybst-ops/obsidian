# 【WorkBuddy实战】创建自己的微信读书专题站

##  效果
https://dracoread.aigc.green/

##  步骤：
•       在WorkBuddy的“专家·技能·连接器”的“技能”tab下搜索“微信读书”，点击“+”安装

![图片](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_png/0m9F5vC1OGjiar77qUcsx83icObprIvEoeIquHBUUOt8IwFeU8TciagsUiaoKIrjVZxlbegcNiaMYrDRp0kzKdGFKoWa4nx8icWgbMkvYZo1hrUHw/640?from=appmsg)

•       回到对话框，输入“/”，选择“微信读书助手”

![图片](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_png/0m9F5vC1OGjoVHkCcP6b0EWKGw4vpUlNiaI0bJxBaPL6HDlsUkKTdDgd3xxjLoXlvHZE3pIa9A0IvPKK5aAzCJribGxcpiaG2l6o5NgR5AP2kw/640?from=appmsg)

•       让WorkBuddy熟悉一下微信读书助手这个skill：

![图片](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_png/0m9F5vC1OGhsXiauM1xVwRHmR8M9z0JKHQsyNJyicPDJuANVMnegm73aoh3s60zXHh79CYfcAPvkiaAE455BicMIA2gvzZXuIJHibZiaVJ74Z1lwQ/640?from=appmsg)

•       WorkBuddy会反馈它缺少API KEY

![图片](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/0m9F5vC1OGjlcgQxlbAHP6SaRA61k3puMr2kWz4VKkJfygOD5ibnSXvS8ibgLIPckysDFNNsbyxuGeWPCYDqCVVtPTuMfUuC1Tt5OL1Y27VdM/640?from=appmsg)

•       访问微信读书Skill页面：   https://weread.qq.com/r/weread-skills     点击“快速配置”：

![图片](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_png/0m9F5vC1OGjZRrsBb43fNKCYTt3DElch4Fiac2ibHmbn43gdKibx4jXEKZl8oFj850ibj2t343xcbjAMGPVb74vQoqhyAFaJpou9BN5Ejcic0OB4/640?from=appmsg)

•       点击“登录微信读书”

![图片](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_png/0m9F5vC1OGiaI75NDvWbAeWNakxHslsjW0TD4ia7fHwdhPMEFPNCwZ3fdwDF5d1PibmTKEQI05icgkEdc2vOo8hWXcIGMkb2tbYed5ib74BUuiaMg/640?from=appmsg)

•       登录成功之后把API KEY复制出来：

![图片](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/0m9F5vC1OGgClV9sQic7XKKxvgc9BC7VibzhOj9lQIT8AePC6qcXAxICpJI06ibnjWr0UDGAnLqpegtTquw9oARnNHDDsrovnLvuLJ3xCRdCPc/640?from=appmsg)

•       打开电脑的命令行终端，按照WorkBuddy给出的要求

-

echo  &#x27; export  WEREAD_API_KEY=wrk-你的apikey&#x27; >> ~/.zshrc     source  ~/.zshrc     echo   "已写入，前缀:    ${WEREAD_API_KEY:0:8}   ..."

![图片](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_png/0m9F5vC1OGgMqOUT3wiaSichlqicWRp1dF95TiaCQnCicS6QNoxStgSFN4rftEXZ7M5AztkqbbYMELLvT8sx87AfAszE2elVMNSaB7WrZoSLyGwo/640?from=appmsg)

•       然后让WorkBuddy看看是否能查到自己的微信读书相关记录了

![图片](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/0m9F5vC1OGjQDz7WG39xByViaRAVPO7jcEGA9o14dEcibrUrRZRgM3vQOnYKv1Y1s3iaawztZ5oY9HdFI1gXGBWoeia9UKQh3zRPictuYDia3NFKg/640?from=appmsg)

•       让WorkBuddy制作一个网站

请你根据我的微信读书记录给我创建一个读书网站+看板，督促我持续在微信读书上持续读书
