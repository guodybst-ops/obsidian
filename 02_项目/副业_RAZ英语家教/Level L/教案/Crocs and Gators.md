## 正文逐段知识点

### What Are Crocodilians?

> All crocodiles, alligators, caimans, and gharials are called ==crocodilians==. These animals are fierce ==hunters==. They eat other animals. A crocodilian's body is built for hunting. It has short, strong legs and a long, powerful tail. It has sharp teeth and strong ==jaws==.

### Kinds of Crocodilians

> Alligators have wide, round snouts. Crocodiles have longer, narrower snouts. They also have two large teeth that stick out. All crocodilians have short legs, and long bodies and tails.
> Caimans live in Central and South America. Gharials are fish-eaters that live in India. They have long, narrow snouts.

**🌍 背景知识**
- 区分鳄鱼和短吻鳄的简易方法：短吻鳄（alligator）的嘴是**U 形**（宽圆），鳄鱼（crocodile）的嘴是**V 形**（窄长）。另外，鳄鱼闭嘴时下颚的第四颗大牙会露在外面。

### Hunting

> Crocodilians are fierce hunters. They use strong jaws to capture their ==prey==. They eat animals as big as deer and cattle, and as small as fish and birds. They cannot ==chew== their food. They either swallow it whole or tear it apart. Sometimes they drag an animal underwater to drown it.

### Swimming

> Crocodilians use large, powerful tails and webbed feet to move through water. When underwater, they close their ears and nostrils so that water stays out. They can also shut off their ==windpipe==. This allows them to open their mouth without breathing in water.

### Babies

> Some crocodilians lay their ==eggs== in sand. Others make a ==nest== with soil and plants. Newly hatched babies are mostly helpless. Many get eaten by other animals. The lucky ones have Mom to protect them after they ==hatch==.

### Protection

> Around the world, people are helping to protect crocodilians. They put eggs in ==incubators== to keep them safe. People then care for the babies until they grow strong enough to survive in the wild. Most crocodilians are ==endangered==. In many parts of the world, people kill them for food and for their skins.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| incubator | /ˈɪŋkjəbeɪtər/ | n. 孵化器 |
| endangered | /ɪnˈdeɪndʒərd/ | adj. 濒危的 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| care for | care for + **n./sb** | 照顾；关心 |
| grow strong enough to | grow + **adj.** + enough + to do | 长大到足以…… |
| in the wild | — | 在野外 |
| kill for | kill + **n.** + for + **n.** | 为了……而杀害…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **make sure + that + 从句**（省略 that）确保……
- **until + 从句**：直到……

**🌍 背景知识**
- 全球有 23 种鳄目动物，其中很多被列为濒危物种（endangered），主要是因为**栖息地破坏**（habitat loss）、**非法捕猎**（poaching）和**贸易**（trade in crocodilian skins）。

## 综合拓展活动建议

1. **对比分类**：用韦恩图（Venn diagram）对比鳄鱼（crocodile）和短吻鳄（alligator）的异同。
2. **口语表达**：用 *either...or...* 和 *allow...to...* 各造一个句子。
3. **跨学科（生物）**：鳄鱼为什么需要"晒太阳"（basking）？它们在生态系统中的作用是什么？
4. **保护意识**：查一查你所在地区有没有濒危动物，它们的生存受到哪些威胁？
5. **仿写训练**：用文中的方法介绍另一种动物（如猫和狗的区别）。
