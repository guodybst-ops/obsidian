## 正文逐段知识点

### Setting the Scene

> Once upon a time, in a town not so far away, there lived a boy named Mateo. And he loved baseball. But Mateo lived with his two older twin brothers. They made Mateo do all the ==chores== while they practiced baseball for the big tryouts.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| chore | /tʃɔːr/ | n. 家务杂事；日常琐事 |
| twin | /twɪn/ | n. 双胞胎之一 |
| tryout | /ˈtraɪaʊt/ | n. 选拔赛；试演 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| Once upon a time | — | 从前（故事开头经典用语） |
| there lived... | There lived + **n.** | 住着……（there be 变体） |
| make sb do sth | make + **sb** + do **sth** | 让某人做某事 |
| practice for | practice for + **n.** | 为……练习 |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **make + sb + do sth**（使役动词 make 后面用省略 to 的不定式）
  - 文中：*They made Mateo do all the chores.*
  - 抽象：**make + 人 + 动词原形**

### The Magic

> Then Coach Wiley appeared. "I saw you throw that ball," he said. "You have an amazing arm." Mateo wished he could go to the tryouts. The coach smiled and said, "How would you like to try out for the team?" Mateo's face lit up!

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| coach | /koʊtʃ/ | n. 教练 |
| team | /tiːm/ | n. 队；团队 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| How would you like to...? | How would you like to do **sth**? | 你想……吗？（礼貌邀请） |
| face lit up | **one's face** lights up | 某人脸上亮起来/喜形于色 |
| try out for | try out for + **n.** | 参加……的选拔 |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **wish + 过去时（虚拟语气）**：表示不太可能实现的愿望
  - 文中：*Mateo **wished** he **could** go to the tryouts.*
- **How would you like to...?** 礼貌邀请/询问

### The Transformation

> Mateo grabbed his ball and glove and ran to the tryouts. He threw the ball as hard as he could. Everyone watched as it flew all the way across the field. He hit a home run! Then he ran to first, second, third, and home = faster than anyone had ever run before.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| grab | /ɡræb/ | v. 抓起；夺过 |
| glove | /ɡlʌv/ | n. 手套（棒球手套） |
| home run | /hoʊm rʌn/ | n.（棒球）本垒打 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| as hard as one can | as **adv.** + as + **sb** + can | 尽可能……地 |
| all the way | — | 全程；一路 |
| faster than | **比较级** + than | 比……更快 |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **as + adv./adj. + as + sb + can/could**：尽可能……地
  - 文中：*...as hard as he could.*
- **比较级 + than + anyone had ever done**：比曾经任何人都……（最高级含义的另一种表达）

### The Twins' Disbelief

> The coach invited Mateo to the team party the next day. The twins saw Mateo at the party and were very angry. "Our little brother joined the team before we did?" they cried. But the coach pretended not to hear them. Mateo was on the team.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| invite | /ɪnˈvaɪt/ | v. 邀请 |
| party | /ˈpɑːrti/ | n. 聚会；派对（基础词） |
| pretend | /prɪˈtend/ | v. 假装 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| invite sb to | invite + **sb** + to + **n.** | 邀请某人去/参加 |
| before we did | before + **从句** | 在我们……之前（did 代指 joined） |
| pretend not to | pretend not to do **sth** | 假装不…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **before we did** = before we joined。英语中可以用 **did** 替代前面出现过的动词，避免重复。
- **pretend + to do / not to do**

### The Shoe Test

> Finally, the coach arrived at Mateo's house. The twins almost knocked each other over in a rush to try on the shoe. No matter how hard they tried, the shoe was still too small. Then the coach spotted Mateo. "Come here, my boy, and try on this shoe." When the shoe slipped on perfectly, they both gasped, "Cinderello!"

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| spot | /spɑːt/ | v. 发现；看见 |
| gasp | /ɡæsp/ | v. 倒抽一口气；惊叹 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| knock each other over | — | 把对方撞倒 |
| in a rush | — | 匆忙地；急切地 |
| try on | try on + **sth** | 试穿 |
| No matter how... | No matter how + **adj./adv.** + 从句 | 无论多么…… |
| slip on | slip on + **sth** | 一下子穿上（很顺滑地穿上） |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **No matter + 疑问词**：无论……
  - 文中：*No matter how hard they tried...*（无论他们怎么试……）
- **when + 从句（一般过去时），主句（过去时）**

**🌍 背景知识**
- 这里是对经典灰姑娘"水晶鞋试穿"桥段的棒球版改编——"试穿棒球鞋"代替了"试穿水晶鞋"。
- 标题 **Cinderello** 是 Cinderella 的变体，-ello 是西班牙语/意大利语的阳性后缀，暗示故事是男性主角。

### The Ending

> "Mateo will be our captain, and he will lead us to victory!" the coach announced. Turning to the brothers, the coach added, "And you two will be our towel boys!"

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| captain | /ˈkæptɪn/ | n. 队长；船长 |
| victory | /ˈvɪktəri/ | n. 胜利 |
| announce | /əˈnaʊns/ | v. 宣布；通告 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| lead sb to victory | lead + **sb** + to + **n.** | 带领某人走向胜利 |
| turn to sb | — | 转向某人 |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **will + 动词原形**（一般将来时）：*will be / will lead*
- **Turning to...**（现在分词作状语）：表示"一边转身一边说"

## 语法句式汇总

| 句式 | 抽象结构 | 文中例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| make sb do | **make + sb + 动词原形** | made Mateo do all the chores | 使役动词+宾语+原形动词 |
| wish + 虚拟 | **wish + 过去时态** | wished he could go | 但愿（与现实相反） |
| as...as one can | **as + adv./adj. + as + sb + can** | as hard as he could | 尽可能 |
| No matter + 疑问词 | **No matter + how/what/where + 从句** | No matter how hard they tried | 无论…… |
| 现在分词作状语 | **V-ing, 主句** | Turning to the brothers, the coach added... | 一边……一边…… |
| will + 动词原形 | **will + do** | will be / will lead | 一般将来时 |

## 综合拓展活动建议

1. **对比阅读**：对比 Cinderello 和经典灰姑娘故事，哪些部分改编了？哪些保留了？
2. **口语/写作**：如果你是 Mateo，你会对 twins 两兄弟说什么？
3. **仿写训练**：用 *make sb do* / *as...as one can* / *No matter how* 各造一个句子。
4. **文化拓展**：了解棒球的基本规则（strike, ball, home run, pitcher 等术语）。
