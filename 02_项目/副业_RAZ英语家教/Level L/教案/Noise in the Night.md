## 正文逐段知识点

### Strange Noises

> Bailey could not fall ==asleep==. He heard a strange noise. It sounded like someone was in the kitchen. *Creak! Creak!* "What was that?" Bailey ==whispered==. He was too scared to get out of bed.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| asleep | /əˈsliːp/ | adj. 睡着的 |
| creak | /kriːk/ | n. 吱吱嘎嘎的声音 |
| whisper | /ˈwɪspər/ | v. 低语；悄悄说 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| fall asleep | — | 入睡 |
| too scared to do | too + **adj.** + to do **sth** | 太……以至于不能…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **too + adj. + to do sth**：太……而不能做某事
  - 文中：*He was too scared to get out of bed.*
  - 抽象：**be + too + adj. + to do sth**

### The Investigation

> Bailey crept down the stairs. The noise was getting louder. He peeked around the corner. His dad was in the kitchen eating ==cereal==! *Creak! Creak!* was the sound of Dad chewing his cereal.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 | 文中变形 |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| cereal | /ˈsɪriəl/ | n. 麦片；谷类食品 | cereal |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| creep down | creep down + **n.** | 蹑手蹑脚下（楼） |
| peek around the corner | — | 在角落偷看 |
| getting louder | get + **比较级** | 变得更大声 |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **get + 比较级 + and + 比较级 / get + 越来越……**
  - 文中：*The noise was **getting louder**.*（声音越来越大了）
  - 同义结构：*get bigger and bigger / get taller*

### The Truth

> Bailey laughed. All that noise was just Dad eating his cereal! He went back to bed. This time, he fell asleep right away. The next morning, Dad said, "I had the strangest dream. I dreamed someone was watching me eat my cereal."

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| strange | /streɪndʒ/ | adj. 奇怪的；陌生的 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| right away | — | 立刻；马上 |
| watch sb do sth | watch + **sb** + do **sth** | 看着某人做某事 |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **watch/see/hear + sb + do sth**：感官动词 + 宾语 + 动词原形（表示看见/听见全过程）
  - 文中：*watched me eat my cereal*

## 语法句式汇总

| 句式 | 抽象结构 | 文中例句 | 说明 |
