## 正文逐段知识点

### Flying in the Moonlight

> It's a quiet, moonlit night. A shadow crosses your path, but you hear no sound. You look up as a dark shape floats over you. It's an ==owl==! More than two hundred kinds of owls live in the world.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| owl | /aʊl/ | n. 猫头鹰 |
| shadow | /ˈʃædoʊ/ | n. 影子；阴影 |

---

### Eyes for the Night

> Owls have very large eyes. Their eyes let in more light than human eyes. This helps them see in the dark. Owls cannot move their eyes. They must turn their whole head to look around. An owl can turn its head almost all the way around.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| human | /ˈhjuːmən/ | n./adj. 人；人类的 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| let in | let in + **n.** | 让……进来；透入 |
| turn all the way around | — | 转过一整圈 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- 猫头鹰的**眼球是管状**（不能转动），但可以通过**转动头部 270°**（几乎一圈）来观察四周。这是因为它们有 14 节颈椎（人类只有 7 节）。

### Even Better Ears

> Owls have excellent hearing. Some owls have ears at different heights on their head. This helps them figure out exactly where a sound comes from. They can hunt in complete darkness using only their hearing.

### Silent Hunters

> Owls are silent hunters. Their ==feathers== have special edges that ==muffle== sound. When an owl flies, it makes almost no noise. This helps them ==sneak== up on their ==prey==.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| feather | /ˈfeðər/ | n. 羽毛 |
| muffle | /ˈmʌfl/ | v. 消音；压低 |
| sneak | /sniːk/ | v. 潜行；偷偷接近 |
| prey | /preɪ/ | n. 猎物 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| sneak up on | sneak up on + **sb/sth** | 悄悄接近…… |
| make (almost) no noise | — | （几乎）不发出声音 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- 猫头鹰的**无声飞行**是因为翅膀羽毛前缘有**锯齿状边缘**（serrated edge），可以打破空气涡流，消除飞行噪音。
