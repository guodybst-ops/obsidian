## 正文逐段知识点

### Introduction

> Work is done when something is **moved from one place to another** ==Machines== are **used to** do the work. They move things from place to place. Some things are easy to move. Other things are not.
> Small ==machines== are **used for** smaller jobs. Things like ==shovels== are simple machines. They do not have many moving parts.
> Big machines are **used to** move heavy things. They are **used to** build tall buildings. They are **used to** build roads and bridges. They are **used to** take ==resources== from the earth.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| machine | /məˈʃiːn/ | n. 机器；机械 |
| shovel | /ˈʃʌvəl/ | n. 铲子；铁锹 |
| resource | /ˈriːsɔːrs/ | n. 资源 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| be used to do | be used to do **sth** | 被用来做…… |
| be used for | be used for + **n./doing** | 被用于…… |
| move from A to B | move + **sth** + from **A** to **B** | 把……从 A 移动到 B |
| simple machine | — | 简单机械（物理概念） |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **被动语态 be + 过去分词**
  - 文中：*Work is done / Machines are used / Things are moved*
  - 抽象：**be + 过去分词 (+ by sb)**，主语是动作的承受者
- **Some..., others...** "一些……另一些……"
  - 文中：*Some things are easy... Other things are not.*

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Simple machine（简单机械）** 是物理概念，指最基本的不使用外部动力的机械装置，如杠杆（lever）、滑轮（pulley）、斜面（inclined plane）、楔（wedge）、螺丝（screw）和轮轴（wheel and axle）。文中提到的 shovel（铲子）就是杠杆的一种。

### Building Tall Buildings

> It takes many big machines to build a tall building. Large power shovels called ==excavators== dig deep holes in the ground. Some power shovels have huge ==buckets== that can scoop a swimming pool-sized pile of dirt in one scoop. Others have clamlike buckets that take big bites out of the earth.
> ==Bulldozers== are machines that move the dirt around. Giant ==dump trucks== carry the dirt away. ==Concrete== trucks carry ==concrete== to the work site. Flatbed trucks carry ==steel== ==beams== and rods to the work site. Large ==cranes== lift steel beams into place. They also carry buckets of concrete to workers high above ground.

> *A bulldozer can move more dirt in fifteen minutes than a person can shovel in five days.*

| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| excavator | /ˈekskəveɪtər/ | n. 挖掘机；挖土机 |
| bucket | /ˈbʌkɪt/ | n. 铲斗；桶 |
| bulldozer | /ˈbʊldoʊzər/ | n. 推土机 |
| dump truck | /ˈdʌmp trʌk/ | n. 自卸卡车；翻斗车 |
| concrete | /ˈkɑːnkriːt/ | n. 混凝土 |
| steel | /stiːl/ | n. 钢 |
| beam | /biːm/ | n. 梁；横梁 |
| crane | /kreɪn/ | n. 起重机；吊车 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| take...to do | It take(s) + **n.** + to do **sth** | 做某事需要…… |
| carry away | carry + **sth** + away | 把……运走/搬走 |
| lift into place | lift + **sth** + into place | 把……吊装到位 |
| high above ground | — | 在地面以上高处 |
| swimming pool-sized | — | 游泳池大小的（复合形容词） |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **called/named + 名词**：表"被称为……的"
  - 文中：*power shovels **called** excavators*（称为挖掘机的动力铲）
  - 结构：**名词 + called/named + 名称**
- **It takes + n. + to do sth**：做某事需要某物
  - 文中：*It takes many big machines to build a tall building.*
  - 抽象：**It take(s)/took + n. + to do sth**
- **that 定语从句**：*...buckets **that** can scoop...*

**🌍 背景知识**
- 文中提到的 excavator（挖掘机）、bulldozer（推土机）、dump truck（自卸卡车）、crane（起重机）是现代工程建设中最常见的四大工程机械。在中文里常统称为"工程机械"（construction machinery/equipment）。
- "swimming pool-sized"是一种生活化的比喻，让小朋友直观理解铲斗有多大。

### Building Roads

> It takes many kinds of large machines to build a road. Large ==scrapers== are used to get rid of trees and brush. They also are used to get rid of small hills. ==Loaders== are used to dig up dirt and rocks. They put dirt and rocks into giant dump trucks that carry it away.
> Some dump trucks are so big that they can carry enough dirt to equal the weight of twenty elephants.
> ==Graders== smooth out the roadway, and ==pavers== lay the road surface onto the roadway. A ==roller== following behind the paver presses down on the new road surface to make it smooth and flat.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| scraper | /ˈskreɪpər/ | n. 铲运机；刮土机 |
| loader | /ˈloʊdər/ | n. 装载机 |
| grader | /ˈɡreɪdər/ | n. 平地机 |
| paver | /ˈpeɪvər/ | n. 摊铺机 |
| roller | /ˈroʊlər/ | n. 压路机；滚轮 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| get rid of | get rid of + **sth/sb** | 清除/摆脱…… |
| dig up | dig up + **sth** | 挖出…… |
| smooth out | smooth out + **sth** | 把……弄平 |
| press down on | press down on + **sth** | 用力按压…… |
| so...that... | so + **adj.** + that + 从句 | 如此……以至于…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **so...that... 结构**：如此……以至于……
  - 文中：*...so big **that** they can carry enough dirt...*
  - 抽象：**so + adj./adv. + that + 从句**
- **现在分词作后置定语**
  - 文中：*A roller **following** behind the paver* → 跟在摊铺机后面的压路机
  - 抽象：**名词 + 现在分词短语**

**🌍 背景知识**
- 筑路工序通常为：**清除(scraper)** → **挖掘(loader)** → **运输(dump truck)** → **平整(grader)** → **摊铺(paver)** → **压实(roller)**。

### Taking Resources from the Earth

> There are many resources we need that are buried deep inside the Earth. We need big machines to get resources out. ==Oil drilling rigs== are set up to remove oil. Large ==drills== are used to get ==coal== out of ==mines==. Very large excavators dig up the earth in search of resources. Huge dump trucks then carry the resources to where they can be used to make products.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| oil drilling rig | /ɔɪl ˈdrɪlɪŋ rɪɡ/ | n. 石油钻机；钻井平台 |
| drill | /drɪl/ | n. 钻机；v. 钻孔 |
| coal | /koʊl/ | n. 煤 |
| mine | /maɪn/ | n. 矿；矿井 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| be buried deep inside | be buried deep inside + **sth** | 被深埋在……里面 |
| get...out | get + **sth** + out (of + **sth**) | 把……（从……）弄出来 |
| set up | set up + **sth** | 建立/架设 |
| in search of | in search of + **sth** | 寻找…… |
| be used to make | be used to do **sth** | 被用来做……（复习） |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **省略关系代词的定语从句**
  - 文中：*resources (that) we need* → that 在从句中作宾语时可以省略
- **where 引导的定语从句**
  - 文中：*...where they can be used to make products* → 在这里where指代"到需要的地方"

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Oil drilling rig（石油钻机/钻井平台）** 有陆地上（land rig）和海上（offshore rig）两种。海上钻井平台可以是固定式的（fixed platform）或浮动式的（floating rig）。
- Coal（煤）→ Oil（石油）→ Natural Gas（天然气）是三大化石燃料。文中提到的"从地下取出资源"同时涵盖矿物和化石燃料。

### Big Machines on Water

> There are big machines that move things on the water. ==Tugboats== are machines that move other things over water by pulling and pushing. They pull ==barges==. They push and pull large ships.
> ==Ocean liners== are large ships that move people. ==Oil tankers== are huge ships that carry massive amounts of oil. And ==container ships== are stacked high with containers that carry things from place to place.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| tugboat | /ˈtʌɡboʊt/ | n. 拖船 |
| barge | /bɑːrdʒ/ | n. 驳船；平底船 |
| ocean liner | /ˈoʊʃən ˈlaɪnər/ | n. 远洋客轮 |
| oil tanker | /ɔɪl ˈtæŋkər/ | n. 油轮 |
| container ship | /kənˈteɪnər ʃɪp/ | n. 集装箱船 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| by pulling and pushing | by + **doing** | 通过拉和推的方式 |
| massive amounts of | massive amounts of + **n.** | 大量的…… |
| be stacked high with | be stacked high with + **n.** | 高高堆满…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **There be + n. + that + 定语从句**
  - 文中：*There are big machines **that** move things on the water.*
  - *Tugboats are machines **that** move other things...*
- **by + doing**：表示"通过……方式"

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Tugboat（拖船）** 虽然体积小，但马力极大，用于在港口引导大船进出港。
- **Container ship（集装箱船）** 是现代全球贸易的核心运输工具。最大的集装箱船可运载超过 20,000 个标准集装箱（TEU）。
- **Oil tanker（油轮）** 载重吨位通常以万吨计，是海上石油运输的主力。

### Big Machines in the Air

> Large ==airplanes== carry people and ==cargo==. A 747 is a giant passenger ==jet== that can carry 400 to 450 people. The C-5 is a huge cargo plane. It is almost as long as a football field. ==Helicopters== are flying machines too. The ==skycrane== is a helicopter that is used to lift heavy objects. It can lift 15,000 pounds.
> You can see that many large machines are at work moving things from place to place. But probably the most common is the ==big rig==. The big rig is **made of** two parts—the ==tractor== and the ==trailer==. Big rigs carry goods over roads that wind around the world.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| airplane | /ˈerpleɪn/ | n. 飞机 |
| cargo | /ˈkɑːrɡoʊ/ | n. 货物 |
| jet | /dʒet/ | n. 喷气式飞机 |
| helicopter | /ˈhelɪkɑːptər/ | n. 直升机 |
| skycrane | /ˈskaɪkreɪn/ | n. 空中吊车（大型直升机） |
| big rig | /bɪɡ rɪɡ/ | n. 大型货运卡车（牵引车+挂车） |
| tractor | /ˈtræktər/ | n. 牵引车；拖拉机 |
| trailer | /ˈtreɪlər/ | n. 挂车；拖车 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| as long as | as **adj.** as + **n.** | 和……一样长 |
| be made of | be made of + **n.** | 由……制成（看得出原料） |
| at work | — | 在工作；在运行 |
| carry goods | carry + **n.** | 运送货物 |
| wind around | wind around + **n.** | 蜿蜒环绕…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **as + adj. + as + n. 同级比较**（复习）
  - 文中：*It is almost as long as a football field.*
- **that 引导定语从句**
  - 文中：*...roads **that** wind around the world*
- **You can see that...** "你可以看到……" 引导宾语从句

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Boeing 747**，编号"珍宝客机"（Jumbo Jet），是全球首款宽体远程喷气式客机，最多可载 500+ 人。1970 年投入运营，2023 年停产，是航空史上的标志性机型。
- **C-5 Galaxy** 是美国空军最大的战略运输机之一，机身长约 75 米（≈一个美式橄榄球场长度：120 yards = 109.7 m；但按 end zone 算确实接近）。
- **Big rig（大型卡车）** 也称为 **semi-truck（半挂卡车）** 或 **18-wheeler（18轮卡车）**，是美国公路货运的主力。

## 综合拓展活动建议

1. **分类整理**：把文中提到的机器按功能分类（陆上工程、水上运输、空中运输）。
2. **口语表达**：选一台你感兴趣的机器，用英文介绍它的功能和特点（至少 5 句话）。
3. **仿写训练**：用 *It takes...to...* / *is used to...* / *called...* 各造一个句子。
4. **跨学科（科学/社会）**：想想你身边有哪些大型机械？它们分别用什么能源（汽油/柴油/电力/蒸汽）？
5. **比较阅读**：对比文中所说的"100 years ago"的蒸汽机器和现在的机器，有什么不同？
