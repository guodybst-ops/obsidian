## 正文逐段知识点

### Lost Keys

> Jae came home from school. He put his hand in his pocket. Where were his keys? He had ==lost== them! His parents would not be home for an hour. "What now?" he thought. Just then, Ron, the man next door, walked past. Jae had never really talked to Ron.

| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 | 文中变形 |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| lose | /luːz/ | v. 丢失；输 | **lost** (lose→**lost** 不规则) |
| pocket | /ˈpɑːkɪt/ | n. 口袋 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| come home from | come home from + **地点** | 从……回家 |
| not...for an hour | — | 一小时内不会……（不能用否定形式翻译） |

### Asking for Help

> Jae marched to Ron's door and knocked. "Hi, Ron. I've lost my keys, and my parents aren't home. Could I wait here with you?" "Sure," Ron said. Jae walked inside. To his surprise, Ron's apartment was full of paintings. "Did you paint all of these?" Jae asked. "Yes," Ron said shyly.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| painting | /ˈpeɪntɪŋ/ | n. 画；绘画作品 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| to one's surprise | to + **sb's** + surprise | 让某人惊讶的是 |
| be full of | be full of + **n.** | 充满…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **I've lost...**（现在完成时）：强调对现在造成的影响（我没钥匙进不了门）
- **Could I...?**（礼貌请求）：比 Can I 更委婉
- **full of + n.** "充满……"

### The Discovery

> They looked amazing! Jae could not believe it. Ron was a very talented painter. Jae's parents came home. "Your keys were in your jacket pocket," his mom said. Jae smiled. He had found something more important than keys.

|------|------|-----------|

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| could not believe it | — | 简直不敢相信 |
| more important than | more + **adj.** + than + **n.** | 比……更重要 |

## 语法句式汇总

| 句式 | 抽象结构 | 文中例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| 现在完成时 | **have/has + 过去分词** | I've lost my keys | 强调对现在的影响 |
| Could I...? | **Could I + 动词原形?** | Could I wait here? | 礼貌请求 |
| to one's + n. | **to + sb's + 情感名词** | To his surprise | 让某人（惊讶/高兴等）的是 |
