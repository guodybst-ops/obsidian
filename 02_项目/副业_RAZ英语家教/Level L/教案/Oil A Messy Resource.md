## 正文逐段知识点

### Introduction

> Oil is a very important ==resource==. We use oil for ==energy==. We use it to make gas for cars. We use it to heat buildings. We even use it to make plastic things. Oil comes from below the ground. We use large drills to dig ==wells==. The wells bring oil to the ==surface==.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| resource | /ˈriːsɔːrs/ | n. 资源 |
| energy | /ˈenərdʒi/ | n. 能源；能量 |
| well | /wel/ | n. 井 |
| surface | /ˈsɜːrfɪs/ | n. 表面；地表 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| come from | come from + **n.** | 来自 |
| bring...to the surface | bring + **n.** + to the surface | 把……带到地表 |

---

### How We Get Oil

> Oil is found in many places. Some places are below land, others below the ocean floor. An oil ==rig== is a machine that drills for oil. Some special oil rigs can ==float== on the ocean. They use pipes to bring the oil to the surface.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| rig | /rɪɡ/ | n. 钻机；钻井平台 |
| float | /floʊt/ | v. 漂浮 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- Oil rig（石油钻井平台）分为固定式（fixed）和浮动式（floating）两种。文中提到的 Development Driller III 就是一种浮动式钻井平台。

---

### Problems with Drilling Underwater

> Problems can happen when people drill for oil. An oil rig caught fire and sank. Oil from the well below began to ==leak== into the ocean. When lots of oil leaks, it makes an oil ==spill==. Oil spills cause ==harm== to people and animals. Oil covers birds and other animals. It can kill many plants and animals.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| leak | /liːk/ | v. 泄漏 |
| spill | /spɪl/ | n. 溢出；泄漏 |
| harm | /hɑːrm/ | n./v. 伤害；损害 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| catch fire | — | 着火 |
| sink into | sink into + **n.** | 沉入…… |
| cause harm to | cause harm to + **sb/sth** | 对……造成伤害 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Deepwater Horizon（深水地平线）**：2010 年 4 月，位于墨西哥湾的 Deepwater Horizon 钻井平台起火爆炸，沉入海底。这是美国历史上最大的海上漏油事故，持续 87 天，泄漏约 490 万桶原油。

---

### Other Sources of Oil Spills

> Oil ==tankers== are big ships that carry oil across oceans. If a tanker crashes or sinks, lots of oil can get in the water. It can take years to clean up the mess from a large oil spill.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| tanker | /ˈtæŋkər/ | n. 油轮；罐车 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| crash or sink | — | 撞毁或沉没 |
| take years to | take + **时间** + to do | 花……时间做…… |
| clean up | clean up + **n.** | 清理 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Exxon Valdez（埃克森·瓦尔迪兹号）**：1989 年在阿拉斯加触礁漏油，是美国历史上最严重的油轮漏油事故之一。

---

### What People Think

> Some people think we should not drill for oil under oceans. Others think we can find safe ways. Everyone agrees that oil spills are bad. But oil is also an important resource.

**📝 句式**
- **Some people think..., others think...** "一些人认为……另一些人认为……"
- **Everyone agrees that...** "大家都同意……"
