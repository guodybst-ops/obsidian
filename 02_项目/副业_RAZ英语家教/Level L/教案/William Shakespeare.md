## 正文逐段知识点

### Introduction

> William Shakespeare was a ==poet== and a ==playwright== in England. He was born in 1564. He died in 1616. He wrote many poems and ==plays==. Millions of people around the world still enjoy his work.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| poet | /ˈpoʊɪt/ | n. 诗人 |
| playwright | /ˈpleɪraɪt/ | n. 剧作家 |
| play | /pleɪ/ | n. 戏剧；剧本 |

---

### Shakespeare's Family

> Shakespeare grew up in a town called Stratford-upon-Avon. He was the third of eight children. When he was 18, he married Anne Hathaway. They had three children.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| grow up | — | 长大（phrasal verb） |
| marry | /ˈmæri/ | v. 结婚 |

---

### Shakespeare in London

> Shakespeare moved to London and joined a group of actors. They built a theater called the Globe. Women were not allowed to act on stage. Men dressed up as women to play female roles.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| actor | /ˈæktər/ | n. 演员 |
| theater (theatre) | /ˈθɪətər/ | n. 剧院；剧场 |
| stage | /steɪdʒ/ | n. 舞台 |
| role | /roʊl/ | n. 角色 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Globe Theatre（环球剧院）** 位于伦敦泰晤士河南岸。原建筑 1599 年建成，1997 年重建开放。所有女性角色原由男性扮演。

---

### Shakespeare's Plays

> Shakespeare wrote comedies, tragedies, and histories. *Romeo and Juliet*, *Hamlet*, and *Macbeth* are famous tragedies. *A Midsummer Night's Dream* is a comedy. His plays are about love, jealousy, power, and friendship.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| comedy | /ˈkɑːmədi/ | n. 喜剧 |
| tragedy | /ˈtrædʒədi/ | n. 悲剧 |

---

### Shakespeare's Importance

> Shakespeare invented many English words and phrases. He wrote about what it means to be human. His works are still performed and studied all over the world.

**🌍 背景知识**
- 莎士比亚**发明或首次使用**了超过 1700 个英语单词和短语，如 *bedroom*, *lonely*, *gossip*, *fashionable*。

| 句式 | 抽象结构 | 文中例句 |

| thousands / millions of | **数量 + of + 复数名词** | millions of people |
