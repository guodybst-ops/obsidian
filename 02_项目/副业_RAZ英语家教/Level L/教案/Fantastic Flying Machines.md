## 正文逐段知识点

### History of Flight

> People have always wanted to fly. They watched birds fly, and some tried tying ==wings== to their arms. Two French brothers made the first hot-air ==balloon==. Another man made the first ==glider== that worked. Later, someone made a better glider that could sail in the air for a long time.

| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| wing | /wɪŋ/ | n. 翅膀；机翼 |
| balloon | /bəˈluːn/ | n. 气球 |
| glider | /ˈɡlaɪdər/ | n. 滑翔机 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**

### The Wright Brothers

> The Wright brothers studied all of these ideas. They tested their own ideas. Finally, they ==designed== an ==airplane== that worked. Orville flew the plane for one full minute. It flew for 260 meters. People could fly!

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| design | /dɪˈzaɪn/ | v. 设计 |
| airplane | /ˈerpleɪn/ | n. 飞机 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Wright brothers（莱特兄弟）**：Orville（奥维尔）和 Wilbur（威尔伯），美国发明家，1903 年 12 月 17 日完成了人类首次动力飞行。
- 首飞地点：北卡罗来纳州 Kitty Hawk，飞行距离 36.5 米（书中写 260 米是不同来源数据）。

### Modern Aircraft

> Many planes have ==propeller== engines. Propeller blades spin like a fan's blades. Many modern planes use ==jet== engines. A jet engine forces hot gases in a stream behind it. That ==force== pushes a plane forward.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| propeller | /prəˈpelər/ | n. 螺旋桨 |
| jet | /dʒet/ | n. 喷气式飞机；喷气发动机 |
| force | /fɔːrs/ | n. 力量；力 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| spin like | spin like + **n.** | 像……一样旋转 |
| force...forward | force + **sth** + forward | 推动……前进 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Jet engine（喷气发动机）** 的工作原理是牛顿第三定律：向后喷出高速气流产生向前的推力（thrust）。
