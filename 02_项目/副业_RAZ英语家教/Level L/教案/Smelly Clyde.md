## 正文逐段知识点

### A Clever Dog

> Clyde was a ==clever== dog. He was smart, and he was ==loyal==. He would get the newspaper for his master, Mr. Mora. He would get Mr. Mora's ==slippers==. He could roll over and sit up. He could lie down and shake hands.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| clever | /ˈklevər/ | adj. 聪明的 |
| loyal | /ˈlɔɪəl/ | adj. 忠诚的 |
| slipper | /ˈslɪpər/ | n. 拖鞋 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| roll over | — | 打滚 |
| sit up | — | 坐起来 |
| lie down | — | 躺下 |
| shake hands | shake hands (with sb) | 握手 |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **would + 动词原形**：表示过去习惯性动作
  - 文中：*He would get the newspaper / He would get his slippers*
  - 抽象：**would + do**（"过去总是……"）

### The Problem

> But Clyde had a problem. He was also very ==smelly==. When he walked into a room, everyone knew it. "Clyde! You need a bath!" Mr. Mora would say. But Clyde hated baths.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| smelly | /ˈsmeli/ | adj. 臭的；难闻的 |
| bath | /bæθ/ | n. 洗澡 |

---

### Helping Out

> One day, Mr. Mora's friend came to visit. He was very sad because he lost something important. Clyde sniffed around and found it! Everyone was so happy. "Good boy, Clyde!" they said. Even though he was smelly, Clyde was a very helpful dog.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| helpful | /ˈhelpfl/ | adj. 有帮助的 |
| sniff | /snɪf/ | v. 嗅；闻 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| sniff around | — | 到处嗅探 |
| even though | even though + 从句 | 尽管…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **even though + 从句**：尽管/即使……
  - 文中：*Even though he was smelly, Clyde was a very helpful dog.*
  - 抽象：**Even though + 从句，主句**

---

## 语法句式汇总

| 句式 | 抽象结构 | 文中例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| would + do | **would + 动词原形** | He would get the newspaper | 过去习惯性动作 |
| can/could + do | **can/could + 动词原形** | He could roll over and sit up | 能够（能力） |
| even though | **Even though + 从句，主句** | Even though he was smelly, he was helpful | 尽管…… |
