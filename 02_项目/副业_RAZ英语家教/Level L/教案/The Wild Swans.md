## 正文逐段知识点

### The Curse

> There were once a king and queen who had eleven sons and one daughter named Eliza. But the queen died. A new ==queen== became their stepmother. She was a wicked witch. She turned the eleven princes into wild ==swans== and sent Eliza away to live with an old woman.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| queen | /kwiːn/ | n. 王后；女王 |
| swan | /swɑːn/ | n. 天鹅 |
| witch | /wɪtʃ/ | n. 女巫 |
| wicked | /ˈwɪkɪd/ | adj. 邪恶的 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- *The Wild Swans*（野天鹅）是丹麦作家安徒生（Hans Christian Andersen）的经典童话。故事核心是**沉默的牺牲**——Eliza 为了救哥哥们被迫长时间不能说话。

---

### The Dream

> One night, an old woman told Eliza about some swans she saw by the sea. Eliza went to the sea and found her brothers! But she could not break the spell. The old woman came to her in a dream and said, "You must make eleven ==shirts== from nettles. You cannot speak while you make them. If you speak, the spell will never break."

### The Sacrifice

> Eliza made nettles into thread and began to ==weave==. Day and night she worked. Her hands became sore. But she kept working. People thought she was a witch. They said she must be ==burned== at the stake. Even then, Eliza kept weaving.

**🔗 词组/搭配**

### The Rescue

> Just as the fire was about to be lit, the swans came flying. Eliza threw the shirts over them. The spell was broken! Her brothers turned back into men. They could speak again! And Eliza could finally speak.

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| just as + 从句 | Just as + 从句 | 正当……的时候 |
| be about to be + 过去分词 | be about to be + **过去分词** | 即将被…… |
| throw over | throw + **n.** + over + **sb** | 把……扔/披在某人身上 |
| turn back into | turn back into + **n.** | 变回…… |

## 语法句式汇总

| 句式 | 抽象结构 | 文中例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| must do / must be done | **must + do / be + 过去分词** | must make / must be burned | 必须（情态动词） |
| cannot...while... | **cannot + do + while + 从句** | cannot speak while you make them | 在做某事时不能…… |
| keep + doing | **keep + doing** | kept working / kept weaving | 持续做 |
| just as + 从句 | **Just as + 从句** | Just as the fire was about to be lit | 正当……时 |
| turn into / turn back into | **turn (back) into + n.** | turned them into swans / turned back into men | 变成/变回 |
