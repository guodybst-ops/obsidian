## 正文逐段知识点

### What Is Sign Language?

> People who are ==deaf== or hard of ==hearing== use their hands to make letters and words. This kind of ==communication== is called sign language. Sign language first started in the United States in 1815. An American named Thomas Gallaudet helped create American Sign Language.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| deaf | /def/ | adj. 聋的 |
| hearing | /ˈhɪrɪŋ/ | n. 听力 |
| communication | /kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/ | n. 交流；交际 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Thomas Gallaudet**：美国聋人教育先驱。1817 年在康涅狄格州建立了美国第一所聋人学校。**American Sign Language (ASL)** 有超过 50 万人使用，在美国和加拿大是第三大语言。

### Fingerspelling

> You can ==spell== words by making shapes with your fingers. This is called ==fingerspelling==. Each letter of the ==alphabet== has a different hand shape. You can practice by learning to sign your own name.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| spell | /spel/ | v. 拼写 |
| fingerspelling | /ˈfɪŋɡərˌspelɪŋ/ | n. 手指拼写 |
| alphabet | /ˈælfəbet/ | n. 字母表 |
