### Babouches (Morocco)

> ==Babouches== are leather ==slippers== from Morocco. They have no heels. They are comfortable and easy to slip on and off. In Morocco, people take off their shoes when they enter a home.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| babouche | /bəˈbuːʃ/ | n. 摩洛哥皮拖鞋 |
| slipper | /ˈslɪpər/ | n. 拖鞋 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| slip on and off | — | 穿脱方便 |

### Klompen (Netherlands)

> ==Klompen== are wooden shoes from the Netherlands. Farmers used to wear them to protect their feet. They are still worn for festivals and ceremonies.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| klompen | /ˈklɒmpən/ | n. （荷兰）木鞋=clogs |
| wooden | /ˈwʊdn/ | adj. 木制的 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Klompen** 是荷兰传统木鞋，正式名称是 clogs。荷兰地势低洼潮湿，木鞋可以保持脚部干燥。

---

### Geta (Japan)

> ==Geta== are traditional Japanese shoes. They are made of wood and have two small pieces on the bottom. Geta keep feet above the ground. This helps keep feet clean and dry.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| geta | /ˈɡetə/ | n. 木屐（日本） |
| traditional | /trəˈdɪʃənl/ | adj. 传统的 |

---

### Cowboy Boots (USA)

> ==Cowboy== boots are from the United States. They were first worn by cowboys who rode horses. The pointed toe helps fit into stirrups. The high heel keeps the foot from slipping through.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| cowboy | /ˈkaʊbɔɪ/ | n. 牛仔 |
| boot | /buːt/ | n. 靴子 |
| stirrup | /ˈstɜːrəp/ | n. 马镫 |
