## 正文逐段知识点

### A Famous Bell

> Big Ben is a huge ==bell== **at the top of** a tall clock ==tower== in London, England. The bell is **as heavy as** two elephants. Big Ben, the tower, and the clock are all important. They are **part of** Westminster ==Palace==, which is **home to** ==Parliament==. People all over the world have **heard of** Big Ben.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| bell | /bel/ | n. 钟；铃 |
| tower | /ˈtaʊər/ | n. 塔；塔楼 |
| palace | /ˈpælɪs/ | n. 宫殿 |
| Parliament | /ˈpɑːrləmənt/ | n. 议会；国会 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| at the top of | **sth** at the top of **sth** | 在……的顶部 |
| as heavy as | be as **adj.** as **n./pron.** | 和……一样…… |
| be part of | be part of **sth** | 是……的一部分 |
| be home to | be home to **sth/sb** | 是……的所在地 |
| hear of | hear of **sb/sth** | 听说（某人/某事） |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **as + adj. + as + n./pron.同级比较**：表示"和……一样……"
  - 文中：*The bell is as heavy as two elephants.*
  - 扩展：*This tower is as tall as a 30-story building.*
- **which 非限制性定语从句**：对前文补充说明
  - 文中：*Westminster Palace, which is home to Parliament.*
  - 结构：**主句, which + 谓语 + 其他**

**🌍 背景知识**
- Big Ben 严格来说是**那口钟**的名字，不是钟楼。钟楼原名 Clock Tower，2012 年更名为 Elizabeth Tower。
- 威斯敏斯特宫（Westminster Palace）又称 Houses of Parliament（国会大厦），是英国议会所在地。

### An Old Building

> Westminster Palace has ==several== buildings. The oldest building is Westminster ==Hall==. The hall is **more than** nine hundred years old and **is still used** today. Kings and queens lived at Westminster **until 1512**.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| hall | /hɔːl/ | n. 大厅；礼堂 |
| several | /ˈsevrəl/ | adj. 几个的；数个的 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| more than + 数字 | more than + **num.** | 超过…… |
| is still used | be + **adv.** + 过去分词 | （仍）被……（被动语态） |
| until + 时间 | until + **time/n.** | 直到…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **被动语态 be + 过去分词**
  - 文中：*...is still used today.*
  - 抽象：**be + 过去分词 (+ by sb)**，表示"被（某人）……"

**🌍 背景知识**
- Westminster Hall 建于 1097-1099 年，已有 900 多年历史，是欧洲现存最大的中世纪木顶大厅之一。
- 1512 年，亨利八世（Henry VIII）因火灾搬离，迁至白厅宫。

### Burned Down and Built Up

> A fire in 1834 ==burned== most of Westminster Palace. Six years later, there was a ==search== to find someone to ==build== the palace again. Charles Barry **got the job** (get 首次出现 → **==got==**). It **took** thirty years and a lot of money to build the new palace.

- 1834 年大火几乎烧毁整座宫殿，仅 Westminster Hall 幸存。
- Charles Barry（查尔斯·巴里）和 Augustus Pugin（奥古斯塔斯·普金）负责重建，哥特复兴风格，1840-1870 年。

### Pugin and the Clock

> Barry and Pugin never ==got== **to see** their finished buildings. They died before the work was finished.
> Augustus Pugin ==planned== the clock that is part of the tower. Pugin ==decided== **to make** a clock with four ==faces==—one on each side. Each clock face has 312 pieces of ==glass==.

- 钟楼 1859 年投入使用，此时两人均已去世。

### Westminster Palace Layout

> In building size alone (not counting the surrounding gardens), the Palace of Westminster ==occupies== 32,374 ==square meters== of space in central London. There are 100 ==staircases== and 1,100 rooms!
> Westminster Palace has three towers. For many years, the Victoria Tower was the largest square stone tower in the world. The Elizabeth Tower is the clock tower that holds Big Ben. The Central Tower was planned as a ==chimney== for smoke from ==fireplaces== inside the palace.

### Parliament — House of Commons

> Westminster Palace is home to Parliament. Parliament is where people make ==laws== for the United Kingdom. There are two houses in Parliament. People in the House of ==Commons== help to decide what laws to make. They ==debate== what the people need.

### Parliament — House of Lords

> People in the House of ==Lords== have ==titles==, such as Lord and Lady. Others are important members of the Church of England. The House of Lords **works with** the House of Commons to make laws. When everyone agrees, the laws become ==Acts== of Parliament.

### An Amazing Clock

> Big Ben's clock tells exactly the right time. People **take good care of** it. It takes a long time to clean the clock faces. People clean them every five years with ==soap== and water. With its four faces, Big Ben is the biggest ==chiming== clock in the world.

| chiming | /ˈtʃaɪmɪŋ/ | adj. 报时的（chime 的 -ing 形式） |
| soap | /soʊp/ | n. 肥皂 |
| exactly | /ɪɡˈzæktli/ | adv. 准确地；恰好地 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| tell the right time | tell + **the time** | 报时准确 |
| take good care of | take good care of + **sth/sb** | 好好照顾/保管 |
| It takes...to... | It take(s) + **time** + to do **sth** | 花时间做某事 |
| every five years | every + **基数词 + years** | 每……年一次 |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **最高级**：*...the biggest chiming clock in the world*

**🌍 背景知识**
- 大本钟以精准著称，钟摆上加一枚旧便士可使钟每天快 0.4 秒。
- 四个钟面直径约 7 米，每 5 年肥皂水清洗一次。

### Visiting Big Ben

> People who live in the United Kingdom can go to the top of the Elizabeth Tower and see Big Ben. There, they also **get a great ==view== of** London and can see how the clocks work. Anyone can go to the Houses of Parliament, ==though==. Everyone can take pictures of the clock tower from the outside.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| view | /vjuː/ | n. 景色；视野；观点 |
| though | /ðoʊ/ | adv. 不过；可是（放句末） |

**🔗 词组/搭配**

## 语法句式汇总

| 句式 | 抽象结构 | 文中例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|----------|------|

## 综合拓展活动建议

1. **阅读理解**：从文中找出至少 3 个数字事实（尺寸、时间、人数等），用完整英文句子复述。

3. **跨学科（社会/历史）**：对比英国议会制度和中国的人民代表大会制度，有什么异同？
4. **仿写训练**：用 *as heavy as...* / *It took...to...* / *There are...* 各造一个句子。
