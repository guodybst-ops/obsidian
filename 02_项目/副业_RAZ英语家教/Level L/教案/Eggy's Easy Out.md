## 正文逐段知识点

### The Nickname

> People call me ==Eggy==. But that's not my real name. When my brother Jordan first saw me, he said, "His head looks like an egg!" My head is normal now, but the ==nickname== stuck. Jordan has always liked to throw things, especially at me. Mom says I could catch even before I could walk.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| eggy (nickname) | /ˈeɡi/ | 鸡蛋头（绰号，egg + y） |
| nickname | /ˈnɪkneɪm/ | n. 绰号；昵称 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| stick (stuck) | — | 粘住；固定下来（文中指绰号一直没被忘记） |
| has always liked | have/has always + 过去分词 | 一直喜欢…… |

### The Game

> During the last ==inning==, I signaled for a ==fastball==. The batter hit a ==pop fly==. The ball sailed toward the sun like a rocket ship. "Catch it, Eggy!" the coach shouted. I ==flung== off my mask. Even with the sun in my eyes, I ==nabbed== the pop-up. My team won the game!

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 | 文中变形 |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| inning | /ˈɪnɪŋ/ | n.（棒球）局；回合 |
| fastball | /ˈfæstbɔːl/ | n.（棒球）快球 |
| pop fly | /pɑːp flaɪ/ | n.（棒球）高飞球 |
| fling | /flɪŋ/ | v. 猛扔；猛地脱下 | **flung** (fling→**flung** 不规则) |
| nab | /næb/ | v. 抓到；接住 | nabbed |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| signal for | signal for + **n.** | 发信号示意…… |
| sail toward | sail toward + **n.** | 向……飞去 |
| fling off | fling off + **n.** | 猛地脱下/甩掉 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Catcher（捕手）** 是棒球中蹲在本垒后的防守球员，负责接投手的球并指挥投手投球策略。
- **Pop fly / pop-up（高飞球）** 指击球员打出的高弧线球，容易被接杀（easy out）。

### The Big Moment

> I ==leaped== up and threw off my mask. Just then, some dust blew into my nose. Before I could stop it, my mouth opened wide, and I ==sneezed==. That's when the ball hit my head. I fell to my knees. Before I ==blacked out==, my mitt reached for the ball.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 | 文中变形 |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| leap | /liːp/ | v. 跳跃；猛冲 | **leaped** (规则) |
| sneeze | /sniːz/ | v. 打喷嚏 | sneezed |
| black out | /blæk aʊt/ | v. 昏倒；失去知觉 | blacked out |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| throw off | throw off + **n.** | 甩掉；脱下 |
| before I could stop it | before + **sb** + could do | 在某人能阻止之前…… |
| fall to one's knees | — | 跪倒在地 |
| reach for | reach for + **sth** | 伸手去够…… |

## 语法句式汇总

| 句式 | 抽象结构 | 文中例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| has always + 过去分词 | **have/has always + 过去分词** | has always liked to throw things | 一直（到现在完成时） |
| could + 动词原形 | **could + do** | I could catch before I could walk | 能够（过去的能力） |
| Even with + n., 主句 | **Even with + n., 主句** | Even with the sun in my eyes, I nabbed it | 即使有……也…… |
