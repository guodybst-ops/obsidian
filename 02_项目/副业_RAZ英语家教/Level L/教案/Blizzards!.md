## 正文逐段知识点

### The Great White Hurricane

> In 1888, a terrible ==blizzard== hit the northeastern United States. It became known as the Great White Hurricane. Strong winds and heavy snow lasted for three days. Snowdrifts covered houses and streets. About four hundred people died.

| blizzard | /ˈblɪzərd/ | n. 暴风雪 |
| hurricane | /ˈhɜːrɪkeɪn/ | n. 飓风；暴风 |
| snowdrift | /ˈsnoʊdrɪft/ | n. 雪堆 |

**📝 语法/句式**

**🌍 背景知识**
- 1888 年的"大暴雪"（Great Blizzard of 1888）是美国历史上最严重的暴风雪之一，覆盖美国东北部，纽约市积雪达 15 米，造成约 400 人死亡。

### Extreme Storms

> A blizzard is a severe ==snowstorm== with very cold temperatures, heavy snow, and strong winds. In a blizzard, the wind blows at least 35 miles per hour. The snow blows with it, and it is hard to see. A ==whiteout== is a situation where the snow is falling and blowing so much that people cannot see anything around them.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| snowstorm | /ˈsnoʊstɔːrm/ | n. 暴风雪 |
| whiteout | /ˈwaɪtaʊt/ | n. 白化现象（暴雪中能见度极低） |
| severe | /sɪˈvɪr/ | adj. 严重的；剧烈的 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| at least | — | 至少 |
| so...that... | so + **adj./adv.** + that + 从句 | 如此……以至于……（复习） |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **where 引导定语从句**：*a situation **where** the snow is falling...*
  - 文中：*A whiteout is a situation **where** people cannot see.*
- **so + adj./adv. + that + 从句**（复习）

### Birth of a Blizzard

> Blizzards usually happen in winter when the air is very cold. But cold air alone does not make a blizzard. Warm, moist air must mix with the cold air to create a blizzard. Strong winds also must blow. The warm, wet air above the ocean mixes with freezing cold air over the land.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| moist | /mɔɪst/ | adj. 湿润的；潮湿的 |
| mix | /mɪks/ | v. 混合；掺和 |
| freezing | /ˈfriːzɪŋ/ | adj. 极冷的；冰冻的 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| mix with | mix with + **n.** | 与……混合 |
| alone does not | — | 仅仅……并不……（alone 强调"单靠"） |

**🌍 背景知识**
- 暴风雪的形成需要三个条件：**冷空气**（cold air）+ **暖湿气流**（warm moist air）+ **强风**（strong wind）。这被称为"blizzard recipe"（暴风雪配方）。

### Blizzard Country

> Some parts of the world have more blizzards than others. In the United States, blizzards often occur in the Great Plains and the Midwest. Blizzards also happen near the Great Lakes. The high mountains out west get blizzards, too.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| occur | /əˈkɜːr/ | v. 发生 |
| plains | /pleɪnz/ | n.（pl.）平原 |
| Midwest | /ˌmɪdˈwest/ | n. （美国）中西部 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| occur in | occur in + **location** | 发生在（某地） |
| more...than... | more + **n./adj.** + than + **n.** | 比……更多…… |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Great Plains（大平原）** 指美国中部的大片草原地带，冬季常有暴风雪。
- **Great Lakes（五大湖）** 区因湖面水汽充足，容易形成"lake-effect snow"（大湖效应降雪）。
- **Midwest（中西部）** 指美国中北部地区，以农业和极端天气闻名。

### Being Prepared and Staying Safe

> A ==blizzard== can be very ==dangerous==. People can get lost or hurt. They can get ==frostbite== or become too cold. Governments try to warn people before a blizzard hits. This gives people time to prepare. They make sure they have batteries and flashlights in case of a power loss. Families make sure they have enough food and water to last for several days. Once prepared, people should settle in and wait out the storm. The best place to be in a blizzard is inside.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| dangerous | /ˈdeɪndʒərəs/ | adj. 危险的 |
| frostbite | /ˈfrɔːstbaɪt/ | n. 冻伤 |
| flashlight | /ˈflæʃlaɪt/ | n. 手电筒 |
| battery | /ˈbætəri/ | n. 电池 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| in case of | in case of + **n.** | 以防……；如果发生…… |
| warn sb before | warn sb before + **n./doing** | 在……之前警告某人 |
| get lost / get hurt | get + 过去分词 | 迷路/受伤 |
| wait out | wait out + **n.** | 等待……结束 |
| last for + 时间 | last for + **time** | 持续（时间）（复习） |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **in case of + n.**：表示"以防万一/如果发生……"
  - 文中：*...in case of a power loss.*
- **enough + n. + to do**：足够的……来做某事
  - 文中：*enough food and water to last for several days*

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Wind chill（风寒效应）**：风吹过皮肤时带走热量，使人感觉比实际温度更冷。暴风雪中风寒效应可使体感温度降至零下几十度。
- **Frostbite（冻伤）** 是皮肤和组织因低温冻结受损，常见于手指、脚趾、耳朵、鼻子等暴露部位。
