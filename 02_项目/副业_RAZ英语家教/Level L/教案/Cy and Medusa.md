## 正文逐段知识点

### School Problems

> "Ugh! Medusa turned someone to stone again," a student groaned. Medusa burst into ==tears== and ran away. Cy ==raced== after her. Outside, he found Medusa with her face buried in her hands. "Can I talk to you?" Cy asked. "Go away if you don't want to be turned into a ==statue==," Medusa said. "You looked me in the eye, and I'm fine," Cy said. "Maybe it's my eye. I can't be turned to stone."

| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| tears (cry) | /tɪrz/ | n.（pl.）眼泪；泪水 |
| race | /reɪs/ | v. 疾走；奔跑 |
| statue | /ˈstætʃuː/ | n. 雕像；塑像 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| burst into tears | burst into + **n.** | 突然大哭起来 |
| race after | race after + **sb** | 追赶某人 |
| with her face buried in | with + **n.** + 过去分词 | 脸埋在……（伴随状语） |
| be turned into | be turned into + **n.** | 被变成…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
- **if + 从句（一般现在时），祈使句**
  - 文中：*Go away if you don't want to be turned into a statue.*
- **be turned into + n.**（被动语态）

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Cy = Cyclops（独眼巨人）**，希腊神话中的独眼巨人族。
- **Medusa（美杜莎）**：希腊神话中的蛇发女妖，与她对视的人会变成石头。

- **Monocle** 是只有一片镜片的眼镜，通常用链子固定在衣服上，是 19 世纪欧洲绅士的经典配饰。

### The Gift

> Medusa handed the monocle to Cy. She had wrapped the glass with wire and attached it to a leather ==strap== carved with lightning ==bolts==. "Wow," Cy said, slipping it over his head. "Thanks!" When the bell rang, Medusa and Cy ==headed== out together. For the first time, neither of them stared at the ground.

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| be attached to | be attached to + **n.** | 被系/贴/连接到…… |

**📝 语法/句式**
