### Toothache

> Louie the ==vampire== paced the halls of his castle. "My mouth is killing me!" he screamed. "Let me see," his ==butler== said. "Master, I insist that you see the ==dentist==." "Not the dentist!" Louie ==shrieked==.

### The New Tooth

> The dentist gave him some ==medicine== and he fell asleep. When he woke up, the tooth was already done. Louie felt his new gold fang with his tongue. He loved it! On Halloween, Louie ==dressed up as== a dentist. That night, he was about to fall asleep when he ==jolted== upright. He pulled out the dental ==floss== before closing his ==coffin==.

## 语法句式汇总

| 句式 | 抽象结构 | 文中例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| insist that | **insist + that + 从句(动词原形)** | I insist that you see the dentist | 坚持要求 |
| be about to do | **be about to do sth** | was about to fall asleep | 正要/即将 |
| dress up as | **dress up as + n.** | dressed up as a dentist | 打扮成 |
