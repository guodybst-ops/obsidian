## 正文逐段知识点

### New Year

> People all over the world celebrate the New Year. People use different ==calendars== to tell when a new year starts. Many people celebrate with fireworks and songs. People ==wish== each other good luck.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| calendar | /ˈkælɪndər/ | n. 日历；历法 |
| wish | /wɪʃ/ | v. 祝愿 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| all over the world | — | 全世界 |
| wish each other | wish + **sb** + **n.** | 互相祝愿 |

### Ramadan

> ==Ramadan== is a special month for Muslims. During Ramadan, people ==fast== during the day. They do not eat or drink from sunrise to sunset. After sunset, they eat a small meal. When Ramadan ends, there is a big celebration called Eid.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| Ramadan | /ˈrɑːmədɑːn/ | n. 斋月（伊斯兰教） |
| fast | /fæst/ | v. 禁食；斋戒 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- 斋月是伊斯兰教历的第九个月。开斋节 **Eid al-Fitr** 是斋月结束后的盛大节日。

---

### Diwali

> ==Diwali== is a festival of lights. Hindus, Sikhs, and Jains celebrate it. People light ==lamps== to show that good ==triumphs== over evil.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| Diwali | /dɪˈwɑːli/ | n. 排灯节（印度教） |
| lamp | /læmp/ | n. 灯 |
| triumph | /ˈtraɪʌmf/ | v. 战胜；胜利 |

---

### Kwanzaa

> ==Kwanzaa== is a holiday celebrated by African Americans. It lasts for seven days. People light candles in a ==kinara==. Each night, one candle is lit. Each day stands for a different ==principle==, such as unity or creativity.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| Kwanzaa | /ˈkwɑːnzə/ | n. 宽扎节 |
| kinara | /kɪˈnɑːrə/ | n. （宽扎节用）烛台 |
| principle | /ˈprɪnsəpl/ | n. 原则；准则 |

---

### Holi

> ==Holi== is a festival in India. People throw colored ==powder== and water at each other. Everyone wears white clothes so the colors show up.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| Holi | /ˈhoʊliː/ | n. 洒红节/好利节 |
| powder | /ˈpaʊdər/ | n. 粉末；粉 |
