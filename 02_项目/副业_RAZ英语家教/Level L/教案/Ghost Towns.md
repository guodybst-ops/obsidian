## 正文逐段知识点

### A Deserted Town

> Ghost towns are empty places where people once lived. People often leave these towns because of some ==threat== or because they can no longer find work. Each town has a story to tell.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| threat | /θret/ | n. 威胁 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**

|------|----------|------|
| can no longer | — | 不再能够 |

### Resources Vanish

> Sometimes people leave a town after they ==run out of== a ==resource==. A ==diamond== was found in a desert in Africa. Soon after, a new town was born. Everyone left when the diamonds became hard to find. Many people hunted ==whales== around this island. Whales became harder to find over time. People left.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| resource | /ˈriːsɔːrs/ | n. 资源 |
| run out of | /rʌn aʊt əv/ | 用完；耗尽 |
| diamond | /ˈdaɪəmənd/ | n. 钻石 |
| whale | /weɪl/ | n. 鲸 |

**🔗 词组/搭配**
| 词组 | 抽象结构 | 释义 |
|------|----------|------|
| run out of | run out of + **n.** | 用完…… |
| was born | — | （城镇）诞生 |
| over time | — | 随着时间的推移 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Kolmanskop（科尔曼斯科普）**，纳米比亚的钻石矿鬼城。1908 年发现钻石后兴起，20 世纪 50 年代钻石枯竭后被废弃，如今被沙漠吞没。

---

### Forces of Nature

> Some places become ghost towns because of natural events. A ==volcano== ==erupted== on this Caribbean island. The capital city was almost ==destroyed==. Over half the people have left. The volcano is still active.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| volcano | /vɑːlˈkeɪnoʊ/ | n. 火山 |
| erupt | /ɪˈrʌpt/ | v. 爆发；喷发 |
| destroy | /dɪˈstrɔɪ/ | v. 毁坏；摧毁 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Plymouth, Montserrat（蒙特塞拉特岛普利茅斯）**：1995 年苏弗里埃尔火山爆发，首府普利茅斯被火山灰掩埋，成为现代鬼城。
- **Craco, Italy（意大利克拉科）**：因山体滑坡和地震被废弃的中世纪古镇，常用作电影取景地。

---

### Deadly Land

> In 1986, a nuclear power plant near **Pripyat** suffered a deadly ==explosion==. Radiation made the area unsafe for people. Everyone had to leave their homes forever.

**📖 生词**
| 单词 | 音标 | 词性·释义 |
|------|------|-----------|
| explosion | /ɪkˈsploʊʒn/ | n. 爆炸 |
| nuclear | /ˈnuːkliər/ | adj. 核的；核能的 |
| radiation | /ˌreɪdiˈeɪʃn/ | n. 辐射 |

**🌍 背景知识**
- **Pripyat（普里皮亚季）**：乌克兰城市，1986 年切尔诺贝利核事故后被完全废弃。今天是著名的"鬼城"旅游目的地。
