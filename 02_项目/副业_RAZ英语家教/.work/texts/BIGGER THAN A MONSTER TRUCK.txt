5 6Vehicles That Move  
Things and People
The Duck
The "Duck" is a truck that can  
travel on both land and water.  
On land, the Duck moves around  
on six wheels. In water, the Duck floats like a boat. The Duck can 
carry people to places that are 
hard to reach.Vehicle BridgeThis vehicle is a bridge  that can 
move. Other vehicles can drive over the bridge. When the bridge  
is no longer needed, it folds up onto the vehicle.
Bigger Than a Monster Truck * Level LThis Duck takes people from the sea to the beach.
Soldiers practice using a vehicle bridge.

7 8Cranes
Cranes lift and move heavy things. 
Some cranes help workers build tall 
buildings. Others are used to move 
goods from ships to land.Dump TrucksThe world's biggest dump trucks 
are used at mines . Miners use these 
trucks to carry rocks and dirt. The 
trucks use some of the biggest tires 
in the world. Their tires are twice as 
tall as a person.
Bigger Than a Monster Truck * Level LThis dump truck is the largest truck in the world.
Heavy cranes are big enough to set up structures.

9 10Overland Trains
These trains are used to move 
heavy things to places without 
roads. The trains move on big 
wheels instead of on train tracks. 
Each wheel is taller than a person.Moving Space VehiclesSome of the world's biggest vehicles were made to move things that 
go into space. They carry space 
vehicles on a high platform . These 
vehicles are so wide that they need their own road.
Bigger Than a Monster Truck * Level LOverland trains, which are used to pull large loads, have many cars.This vehicle moves space shuttles at a top speed of .9 miles per hour (1.4 kph).

11 12Vehicles That Dig
Giant Power Shovels
Miners use giant power shovels  
to dig rocks and dirt out of a mine. Giant power shovels have a big 
bucket  that can break into the 
earth and scoop it up.Tunnel Diggers
These vehicles dig under the 
surface of the earth. They can even 
move through solid rock.
Bigger Than a Monster Truck * Level LSome giant power shovels load dump trucks full of sand.
Machines like this one dug a tunnel between England and France.

13 14Bagger 288
The largest vehicle in the world is 
the Bagger 288. This huge vehicle 
digs mines. The Bagger 288 is almost 
as long as two football fields.
It digs with a huge wheel. The wheel has buckets along its edge 
that scoop up dirt as the wheel 
turns. The Bagger 288 is big enough 
to move mountains.
Do You Know?
People have built enormous vehicles since 
ancient times. The Helepolis (heh-LEH-puh-lis) 
was a huge tower built on wheels. It was used  in battles in ancient Greece. The Helepolis was so heavy that more than 3,000 men were needed to move it.
Bigger Than a Monster Truck * Level LThe Bagger 288 moves to a new mine.
The Bagger 288 weighs 13,500 tons.
