4Introduction
I love shoes--all kinds of shoes .  
My grandma loves to travel .  Last 
year, she took a trip around the 
world .  She sent my brother postcards 
from all the places she visited . 
She sent me shoes!
Howdie from Australia
Hello from Holland


===PAGE BREAK===

Shoes Around the World * Level L 5Babouches
First, Grandma sent me some 
babouches (bah-BOO-shes) from Morocco . They are soft and comfortable like slippers . But you can wear them outdoors . Grandma says they are made of camel hair  . 
They are easy to slip on and off .
6Klompen
Next, Grandma sent me some 
klompen (KLOM-pen) from Holland . They make a clip-clop sound when I walk . That's because klompen are made of wood . 


===PAGE BREAK===

Shoes Around the World * Level L 7People used to wear them as work 
shoes on farms and in factories and mines . Today, people wear klompen because they are comfortable and they look nice .
8Geta
In geta (GEH-tah) sandals, I can be 
much taller! 
Grandma sent these tall wooden geta 
from Japan . Both men and women used to wear these sandals with their beautiful silk kimonos . The high wooden platforms kept their kimonos out of the dust and mud . 


===PAGE BREAK===

Shoes Around the World * Level L 9People started wearing wooden 
geta over 2,000 years ago . Today, Grandma says this traditional shoe is usually worn only for special occasions and official ceremonies . 
To wear them, I had to practice 
how to walk in a new way  . But 
after a few tries, I could do it! I just 
imagined I was wearing a long silk 
kimono as I clack-clacked along in my geta!
In cooler weather, geta are often worn with tabi socks.
10Saami Reindeer Boots
Next, Grandma sent me some Saami (SAH-mee) reindeer boots from Lapland . That's the part of Norway, Sweden, Finland, and Russia that is north of the Arctic Circle . 
These boots are very warm . 


===PAGE BREAK===

Shoes Around the World * Level L 11See how the toes turn up? That's to 
help people walk through the snow  . 
The boots tie around your ankle to keep the snow out and the heat in .
12Cowboy Boots
Near the end of her trip, Grandma went to Australia . My cowboy boots came from there . 
Did you know 
cowboy boots are pointed at the toes? That 
is so cowboys 
and cowgirls can easily slip their feet into saddle stirrups . 


===PAGE BREAK===

Shoes Around the World * Level L 13Cowboy boots are tall to protect 
cowboys from cactuses and snakes . I hope Grandma did not run into any snakes in Australia!
14African Sandals
Last, Grandma sent me some 
sandals from West Africa . It's very hot there . That's why these sandals have such thick soles . Thick soles protect your feet from the hot ground . Also, sandals let air move 
around your feet to keep you cool .


===PAGE BREAK===

Shoes Around the World * Level L 15Conclusion
People around the world wear 
different kinds of shoes . They wear different shoes at different times . They wear different shoes for different reasons . Just like me!
